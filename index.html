What we do

Outcomes and performance frameworks

Clear outcomes and measures that speak to your strategy.

The question at the table

"Are we measuring the impact of our organisation, or just counting what we do?"

Deliverables
  • Intervention logic and theory of change
  • Board and commissioning KPI architecture
  • A clear reporting framework

Social Return on Investment

Show the value your work creates to society. This is invaluable for those with accountability to funders or who are trying to actively show how they are changing the world for the better.

The question at the table

"How can we demonstrate to funders, philanthropic trusts and commissioners the impact we are making?"

Deliverables
  • A clear report showing the Social Return on Investment of the organisation
  • Looking at both the financial impact but, equally importantly, the stories of lives that are being impacted
  • Grounded in strong methodology
  • A unique addition to funding business cases and public sector tenders

Ethical AI governance and consulting

Practical oversight, policy and decision rights for boards and executives adopting AI.

The question at the table

"How do we govern AI, factoring in risks and opportunities, and ensure we are using it ethically to drive performance?"

Deliverables
  • Board AI governance charters and policies
  • Data governance frameworks
  • Decision-rights and risk assurance matrices

We are also open to running customised training sessions on how your organisation could start using AI safely.

Open method.
